## TLDR

I engineered an end-to-end satellite image data stream for lossy laser and RF payload links.

The pipeline combined compression, error correction, and encryption so image chunks could survive interrupted or corrupted transfers without wasting scarce bandwidth.

<Metric value="~40%" label="effective throughput gain" />

I also integrated low-level USB/UART drivers on Linux using FTD2XX and Xilinx tooling for custom baud rates and robust streaming.
