## TLDR

I built an interpretable disinformation classifier from a scraped and cleaned dataset of **1,000+ text samples**.

The model used Logistic Regression with GloVe embeddings and Bag-of-Words features, then improved through confusion-matrix driven iteration.

<Metric value="91% / 87%" label="accuracy / F1-score" />

The preprocessing pipeline cut experiment iteration time by about 40%.
