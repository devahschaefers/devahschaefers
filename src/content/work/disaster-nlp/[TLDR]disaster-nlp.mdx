## TLDR

I analyzed roughly **45K disaster-related tweets** across 14 events to pull out location and need signals from messy real-time social media.

The project used RoBERTa and VADER for sentiment, plus geospatial and textual models to identify where attention was most urgent.

Batch processing cut inference time roughly in half, which made model iteration practical on the full dataset.
