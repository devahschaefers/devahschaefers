## TLDR

I worked on decoding gait information from noisy, non-invasive EEG for future BCI-driven mobility systems.

The final approach moved away from pure joint-angle regression and toward a multitask temporal convolutional network that predicts heel-strike events, gait phase, and smoothed ankle angle from 1-second EEG windows.

<Metric value="0.90" label="heel-strike AUC" />

The main lesson: EEG gait decoding depends as much on dataset choice, preprocessing, target definition, and evaluation splits as it does on architecture.
