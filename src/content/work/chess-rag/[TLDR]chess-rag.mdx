## TLDR

An LLM chess coach that uses retrieval to ground move explanations in similar historical positions and concept templates instead of relying on raw model guesses.

I extracted tactical and strategic features from board states, retrieved relevant context, and built an offline evaluation pipeline over **2M+ positions** to measure whether the hints actually helped.

<Metric value="+20%" label="agent performance on the eval set" />

The main win was making coaching quality measurable, not just subjectively nicer.
